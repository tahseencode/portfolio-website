Human Emotion & Drowsiness Detector with Buzzer

Real-time driver fatigue scoring with automated audio alert trigger

If fatigue scores exceed safety thresholds, the system immediately fires a hardware audio buzzer alarm to awaken the driver and avert highway accidents.
